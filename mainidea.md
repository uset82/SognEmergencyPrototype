# Sogn Emergency Coordination — Main Idea

> **Student concept prototype (INN524, HVL). NOT an operational emergency system. All data is fictional.**

## Project name

Sogn Emergency Coordination

## Tagline

One emergency. One shared incident picture. Personal guidance for every person affected.

## Problem scenario

A large passenger ship approaching a port/kai in Inner Sogn (Flåm) may lose manoeuvrability and become a collision risk. Sea, land, emergency actors, medical services, authorities, and civilians may not all share the same situational picture at the same time.

A major accident could involve: ship crew, port/kai, municipality, police, fire and rescue, paramedics, hospitals, rescue helicopters, maritime rescue, public authorities, transport companies, NGOs, tourists, residents, and other civilians.

The project explores whether an integrated professional platform + civilian app + agent orchestration layer could improve preparedness, communication, evacuation, and response.

## Core principle

Agents connect the entire ecosystem. The web platform and mobile app are human interfaces. Underneath them is a network of specialised agents coordinated by one Main Agent / Orchestrator.

## Safety / product constraint

Agents may prepare, distribute, personalise and coordinate information — they must NOT freely invent emergency policy. Danger zones, safe areas, evacuation plans, routes, alert templates and operational instructions in this prototype are represented as **PRE-APPROVED / FICTIONAL DATA**. The AI layer personalises and coordinates predefined information.

## Fictional demo scenario

Location: Flåm / Inner Sogn. A large passenger vessel has lost manoeuvrability while approaching the harbour. Estimated collision risk window: 15 minutes (T-15 → T-1 timeline; see `src/data/scenario.ts` in the website).

## Visual identity

Scandinavian, calm, high-stakes: dark navy / slate, white, emergency red only for critical states, amber for warning, green for safe.
